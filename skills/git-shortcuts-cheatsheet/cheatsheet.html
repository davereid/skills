Watch out when switching platforms

  • Review comments are reversed. GitHub: Mod+Enter posts now. GitLab: it adds to your pending review.
  • c differs. GitHub: new issue. GitLab MR: next commit (i is new issue).
  • Case matters on GitLab. Shift+m = all your MRs; g→m = this project's MRs.
