# project-backlog

Moves the work in a checkout's local notes, such as an uncommitted `BACKLOG.local.md`, to where it lasts: remaining tasks on your open PRs and merge requests, existing issues, or new ones. Items that can't be public, such as client details, stay local.

It shows the whole plan, with every draft, and posts nothing until you approve it. Only for projects you maintain.

Usage: `/project-backlog` in the checkout, or `/project-backlog <notes file>`.
